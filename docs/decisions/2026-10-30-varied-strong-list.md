# A varied list of strong suggestions: at most five from one company at a time

**Date:** 2026-10-30 · **Decided by:** the owner ("if we find more than 5 people that we believe are strong ... from
the same company, we ofcourse should continue, and it is right we find all the strong people from there, however, we
should propose only 5 candidates at the time, and keep the rest in the bank ... but our sourcing should continue with
other companies"; then "yes build it") · **Status:** accepted, built

## Why
The strong list of a role was dominated by the one company that gave most: on Procure AI, 21 of 25 strong people had
been found at Omnea (19 still work there); on Bonaventura, 11 of 20 had been found through FURLA. Omnea's people alone
met Procure AI's target of 15 waiting, so its search stopped before it reached other companies. Approaching a whole
team at one company at once is also a risk for the desk (people talk; it reads as a raid on the company).

## Decisions
1. **At most five strong suggestions from one company at a time** (plan rule `per_company`, 5 by default, 0 for no
   limit; a field under "Frequency, target and budget"). The company is where the person mainly works now: their
   current job started last, never a board seat or volunteering, merged companies as one. The five shown are the best
   of them, in the list's own order (what their own words show of the work first).
2. **The others are held back, never lost:** under the strong suggestions, "16 more strong at Omnea, held back so the
   list stays varied", with their cards one click away. As those shown are decided (chosen, passed, later), the next
   from that company moves up. Possible matches are never held back.
3. **The target counts at most five from one company**, so the search goes on to other companies until the strong
   list is varied; the Sourcing tab says "9 of 15 strong waiting (23 in all: at most 5 from one company count)", and
   each run's plan step in the activity log says how the target was counted.
4. **A company that gave its share is searched again only after every other company**: it still gets its remaining
   titles, last, so its other strong people are still found, as the owner asked.
5. **A changed rule is merged into the plan's rules** (changing one no longer resets the others).
6. Nobody's rating changes: the same people are strong; only what is shown first, and when the search stops, differ.

## Measured on the live desk (2026-10-30, read only)
- Procure AI: 5 of Omnea's 19 shown, 14 held back; the target counts 9 of 23, so a search would go on.
- Bonaventura: 5 strong work at FURLA now (the other 6 found through FURLA have moved on): all shown.
- Ferrari and InSAR: no company above five; nothing changes.

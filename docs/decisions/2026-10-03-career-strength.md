# ADR: career strength is evidence; personality, culture and vibes are never evaluated

**Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Status:** accepted by the owner (deXveYloZper), 2026-10-03.

## Decision
1. Every candidate gets a **career strength** understanding from evidenced facts: relevant experience per role family, progression, stability (contractor-aware), employer type and stage, domain exposure, education, notable achievements. Each dimension shows its evidence and reason; an overall band appears above a coverage floor; a recruiter can override with a reason.
2. **Personality, culture fit and "vibes" are not evaluated, not scored and not modelled.** They cannot be evaluated from the evidence in scope, so the platform has no such features at all.
3. **Education is merit evidence.** University rank (and subject strength, e.g. engineering departments) comes from a cited ranking source and **may carry weight** in strength and matching. It is shown to the recruiter with its source.
4. **Employer type is evidence of environment and ownership** (product start-up, scale-up, unicorn, consultancy, outsourcer) and is weighed by the desk's rules.
5. **Judgement by versioned rules, not by the model:** the model classifies and extracts with citations; code composes levels and bands under a versioned rubric (blueprint E-i10).
6. Patterns that need context (short stints, gaps) also raise call questions; known company events (shutdowns, layoffs) soften them.

## Why
A master recruiter reads a career before a job: relevance of experience, progression, stability, the companies and their stage, the domain, the education. Matching without it is keyword matching. Owner's brief, 2026-10-03; blueprint F7 (General Strength, Job Fit).

# mAIndScout — Blueprint v4
## 02 — Feature Specifications

*v4.2: implement against 05 where 05 revises a mechanism. Feature text below is the target, already patched in place for RA-01–RA-05 and RA-08–RA-10.*

Ten features in dependency order — each consumes the outputs of those before it. Per feature: purpose, governing principle, mechanics (the corrected design stated positively, with rationale where a choice is non-obvious), and interfaces. Schema referenced here is defined in 01 §D.

## Feature 1 — Ingestion & Extraction

**Purpose:** convert any incoming file into an immutable original, a faithful text representation with permanent evidence anchors, a document type, an authority, an effective date, and a dispatch — without silently corrupting, misattributing, or losing information.
**Principle (P1):** never assume one document = one author = one subject = one language = one version.

**Mechanics**
1. **Hash & dedup.** SHA-256; byte-identical files short-circuit (activity recorded; reprocess only if `last_processed_with` is stale). Hash dedup is deliberately weak — *content-level* dedup belongs to the claims layer (F3 stint reconciliation absorbs re-arrivals). Developers must know this layering to avoid both over-engineering here and false assumptions downstream.
2. **Immutable storage; subjects registered separately.** At storage time the subject is unknown (identity hasn't run), so documents are org-scoped and `document_subject` rows are written afterwards by identity/extraction. The erasure cascade depends on this table to distinguish single-subject documents (delete) from multi-subject ones (sever links).
3. **Extraction artifacts.** Text-layer parse + **always** harvest PDF link annotations and mailto/tel (LinkedIn URLs frequently exist *only* as annotations — losing them loses the strongest identity key). Reading-order heuristics (dates detached from headings; sections out of canonical order) or designed layouts set `needs_vision` and, **after Slice 0**, trigger **vision re-extraction** over rendered pages. Slice 0 is text-layer only: designed/two-column/photo documents raise the flag and proceed with known loss rather than a second model (05 D2). Every extraction is a hashed, immutable `extraction_artifact`; **evidence locators point into artifacts, never at bytes** — which also makes retries idempotent (vision is non-deterministic; the artifact is produced once and reused).
4. **Vision bias containment.** CVs contain photos; the text layer never saw them, a vision model does. Three layers: transcription-only prompt (never describe people/images); output validator blocks appearance-derived claims (extends the special-category blocklist); the exclusion is documented in the bias record. This is what makes vision extraction admissible in a high-risk system.
5. **Classification with recovery.** Full taxonomy (01 §D1), confidence-scored; below threshold → `needs_human` Decision instead of dispatch. `doc_type` is **revisable**: a correction re-dispatches and voids wrong-pipeline claims. Downstream agents get a bail-out check ("does this document describe a person?") — agents that cannot refuse wrong input amplify upstream errors.
6. **Multi-subject splitter.** Shortlist PDFs and email threads contain several people. Post-classification, multi-person documents are segmented; each segment routes through identity + extraction independently, all linked to the one stored original via `document_subject.segment_locator`.
7. **Third-party-transformed detection.** Agency-retyped CVs (branding/layout signals) set `transformed_by_third_party`: factual skeleton keeps candidate-authored weight, prose drops toward third-party, **auto-approval disqualified**, and stripped contacts read as "withheld" (ask the agency, not the web).
8. **`as_of` inference.** Precedence: content-stated durations → PDF metadata → upload time; disagreement lowers `as_of_confidence`, which softens recency in reconciliation (B3).
9. **Job-level resolution at dispatch.** JDs are re-sent and revised; matching (hiring company from *content*, never branding + normalized title + recency) yields "new version of job X, or new role?" — a confirmed new version supersedes requirement claims and triggers pool re-matching.
10. **Language.** Detection at extraction; language-aware prompts; text payloads store `{original, normalized_en}` so matching and dedup operate on one language while evidence stays faithful.
11. **Document lifecycle.** `received → stored → extracted → classified → dispatched → processed`, with `needs_human` and `reprocessable` — event-validated like every state machine, so a document can never be half-processed invisibly.

**Interfaces:** produces artifacts + attributed identity signals (F2), documents for extraction (F5), snapshots arrive here from F6; consumes nothing.

## Feature 2 — Identity & Entity Resolution

**Purpose:** decide whether identity signals refer to a known person/company — creating, attaching, merging, or keeping separate — so the knowledge base converges on one entity per real-world entity without ever fusing two different ones.
**Principle (P2):** false split = cheap nuisance; false merge = the catastrophe (claims interleave, scores describe a chimera, and the merged profile looks *rich*, not broken). All thresholds lean toward separate; merging is cheap and human-confirmed.

**Mechanics**
1. **Subject attribution before indexing.** Agency documents carry the *agency's* contacts (footer email, consultant phone); indexed as keys, one recruiter's email deterministically fuses every candidate they ever send. Signals pass attribution (position in document, domain-vs-branding match, `transformed_by_third_party`, name-localpart match); unattributable signals are stored, never matched on. A nightly **frequency tripwire** quarantines any identifier appearing across many unrelated candidates.
2. **The index is the claim graph.** Matching runs as a **live query** over identity/contact claims in the same transaction as the claim write (05 RA-02). A rejected wrong-email claim must stop matching *in the same transaction* — a Postgres materialized view cannot do this, and a shadow identity store drifts and keeps attaching documents via keys a human already rejected. A denormalized `identity_key` table is allowed only if `api/` is the sole writer, including reject/supersede.
3. **Deterministic stage, with sanity.** Exact email/phone/LinkedIn matches attach — after a name-compatibility check (keys are high-precision, not infallible: recycled numbers, shared mailboxes). **Conflicting deterministic keys** (email→A, LinkedIn→B) never auto-attach: Decision + a proposed `SameAsClaim(A,B)`, since the conflict is itself merge evidence.
4. **Fuzzy stage, size-aware.** Name-component matching tolerant of hyphenation, compound/married surnames, honorifics, transliteration; employer/period overlap **weighted inversely to employer size** (two J. Smiths at a global telecom ≈ coincidence; at an 8-person startup ≈ conclusive — same rule for education); embedding similarity as a supporting signal.
5. **Three-band output.** High → proposed SameAsClaim (human confirms). Middle → LLM adjudication; only positive verdicts become proposals. Low → **silently separate** — surfacing every "maybe" floods the Inbox with common names, and separateness is the safe default; the nightly sweep revisits as evidence accumulates.
6. **Not-same memory.** A rejected SameAsClaim persists as a constraint: never re-proposed absent *new deterministic* evidence; fed to the adjudicator as a hard negative; added to eval as a labeled hard case. Humans saying "different people" is a top-value label — never discard it.
7. **Merge = redirect (E-i13).** Claims keep their original subject forever; approval sets `merged_into_id`; reads resolve redirect chains (sweep flattens; cycles prevented). Unmerge = supersede the SameAsClaim — original ownership was never destroyed. Post-merge derived claims record both parents, so unmerge trips the standard cascade. Merge-time **stint reconciliation** runs on the resolved view (else entity resolution reintroduces the duplicates it exists to prevent). The same redirect discipline applies to `candidate_job`, scores, Brief items, threads, and `job.hiring_company_id` (05 RA-03): one pair on the job → redirect; two pairs with compatible state → keep the more advanced row; two pairs with conflicting human-touched state → Decision, no smash of `UNIQUE(candidate_id, job_id)`. Slice 0: if either subject already has a pair row, SameAs is human-only and pair collapse waits. Rationale: merge-as-rewrite destroys the information (which claims were whose) that reversal requires — the blueprint's earlier "reversible" promise was structurally false until E-i13; the pair graph had the same hole until RA-03.
8. **Companies: brand-level canonical entities.** What candidates write ("BT") is the entity; registry IDs (Companies House number — the one truly deterministic company key) attach where unambiguous; parent/subsidiary links are ordinary claims from research; the Critic raises a Decision only when group-vs-unit ambiguity *materially* changes a score. Full corporate-structure modeling is an explicit ADR-deferred non-goal.
9. **Suppression check first.** Erasure writes salted hashes of deterministic identifiers; the identity stage checks the registry before anything else — a hit blocks ingestion of that subject and logs the encounter without personal data. Without this, sourcing re-ingests the person who objected, on a loop.

**Interfaces:** consumes attributed signals (F1); produces canonical IDs for everything; merge events trigger F3 reconciliation and F7 staleness.

## Feature 3 — The Claims Engine

**Purpose:** hold all knowledge as atomic, evidenced, lifecycle-managed claims such that there is always exactly one current belief per fact, every belief is traceable, and no belief changes invisibly.
**Principle (P3):** the record (what sources said) is not the belief (what we currently hold true). The record only grows; beliefs change only through the gate.

**Mechanics**
1. **Stint natural key & re-keying.** `CareerStepClaim` keys on (candidate, canonical company, overlapping period) — but extraction precedes company resolution, so stints are created with a provisional company reference and **re-keyed when resolution completes** (a defined step, not an accident). Keys evaluate through company redirects; the sweep re-runs stint reconciliation whenever a company merge collapses two keys.
2. **Same-stint confidence, boomerangs free.** Attach as an observation only when same-stint is confident (clear overlap given stated precision, or matching title + single-mention context); otherwise create separately with `possible_duplicate_stint` → one lightweight review choice. Wrongly fusing two real stints corrupts tenure math and the career story; wrongly splitting is recoverable — P2's asymmetry, applied here. Leave-and-return employment therefore needs no special case: non-overlapping periods are simply two claims.
3. **Approval pins a snapshot (E-i3).** The resolved contradiction at the heart of this feature: "immutable when approved" vs "new evidence attaches and corroborates" — attaching an observation that changes the reconciled title of an approved stint would mutate a human-approved belief with nobody in the loop. Fix: `approved_view` pinned at approval; new observations always attach to the record; same recomputed view ⇒ silent corroboration (confidence rises); different view ⇒ the approved claim stands and a **proposed revision** (old view, new view, causing observation) goes to review as a diff.
4. **Origin-aware corroboration (B2).** Every evidence row carries origin; the corroboration boost applies only across origins. Rationale: most "independent" sources share one informant — the candidate — and channel-counting inflates confidence precisely on the self-reported claims most worth doubting.
5. **Field-level cascade (E-i5).** `derived_from` records consumed JSON paths; the cascade fires only when a consumed field's reconciled value changes — claim-level dependency produced both noise (dates change → seniority re-review) and blindness. Agents already know what they read; the harness records it (F5.6).
6. **Claim classes.** `observed` (evidence from the world; full lifecycle) / `inferred` (LLM judgment over claims; full lifecycle + cascade) / `computed` (deterministic functions — gaps, tenure totals, consistency checks; **auto-recomputed on input change, never individually reviewed** — the rule is approved once). This solves the negative-space problem (a GapClaim can't `derive_from` the absence of claims; recomputation invalidates it when a stint fills the gap) and removes a whole category from the review queue. Computed timeline-consistency **carves out education ∩ employment** (05 RA-09). Overlapping employment-like stints stay separate and raise concurrency flags on both sides (04 AL-09).
7. **Supersession semantics.** An approved claim remains the official belief until its replacement is *approved*; provisional consumers may read the pending superseder, labeled. All readers resolve chains to heads; `claim_set_hash` is computed over resolved heads (else chain-resolution differences masquerade as real changes).
8. **Per-type dedup policies; idempotent extraction.** Every type declares its natural key in the registry (SkillClaim: candidate + normalized skill; LocationClaim: candidate + place + basis; …). Re-extraction (a designed feature) *replaces* its own still-proposed prior output per (document, ontology version) and routes conflicts with approved output through the revision path — otherwise every model upgrade twins the corpus.
9. **Atomic contradictions; temporal carve-out.** Resolving a contradiction approves one side and rejects/supersedes the other in a single transaction — never two co-approved claims both asserting current truth. Exception offered by the resolver: apparent contradictions that are temporal succession ("Series A" vs "Series B") resolve by bounding both validity windows, both approved. `volatile` types (size, stage, availability) *default* to bounding — routine refreshes of a growing company must not spawn fake contradictions — and the resulting trajectory is cross-linker material.

**Interfaces:** consumes staged claims from F5 commits; feeds F4 items (revisions, duplicates, contradictions), F7 hashes, F9 flags.

## Feature 4 — The Review System

**Purpose:** convert proposed claims and pending decisions into approved beliefs and resolved actions with minimum human attention — while guaranteeing the attention spent is meaningful, measured, and auditable.
**Principle (P4):** attention is scarce, its quality varies (a considered individual approval ≠ a reflexive bulk click), and for an EU high-risk system the oversight must be *demonstrably* real, not ceremonial.

**Mechanics**
1. **One source of truth.** Lifecycle state lives only on the claim; a Decision is a presentation/grouping object whose status derives from its members. **Dismiss ≠ reject:** dismissing snoozes (resurface date); rejection is only ever an explicit per-claim act. Without this rule the first developer shortcut creates claims simultaneously approved and awaiting review.
2. **Batches seal on first interaction.** An open decision absorbs late-arriving items (enrichment lands an hour after review) until the operator opens it; from then it is sealed, and later arrivals accumulate into the next batch, surfaced when aged or sized enough. Kills both fragmentation and reopened-batch confusion.
3. **Attention grades + policy audit sampling.** Every approval records `policy | human_bulk | human_individual`; learning weights labels by grade. A random ~5% of policy auto-approvals resurfaces as ordinary review items — when sampled items start getting overturned, the policy is drifting and the system proposes tightening. This converts "trust the policy" into "continuously verify the policy" — better engineering, and exactly the human-oversight evidence an AI Act audit asks for.
4. **Policy lifecycle.** Versioned; **tightening offers a sized recall** ("this recalls 214 claims — proceed?") of past approvals under the loose version; loosening is never retroactive; predicates **fail closed** (evaluation error → individual review). An approval gate that fails open isn't a gate.
5. **Inbox ranks by decision value, not candidate strength.** Ranking purely by provisional score starves thin-document candidates forever (unreviewed → no official score → no matching → buried) — the bias channel closed in scoring, reopened through queue order. Priority = provisional promise + staleness boost + **unblocking effect** (does this resolve a gate — an identity merge, an active-job match, an outreach precondition?) + job urgency, with a hard floor (nothing unreviewed beyond N days). Identity confirmations rank intrinsically high.
6. **Derivation-bundle review.** An inferred claim renders with its consumed parents inline (known via field-level provenance); one considered action approves the bundle, every member explicitly listed and individually logged. Not implicit approval — visible endorsement of a coherent story, and an attention win. Validation: an inferred claim can never be approved over a rejected parent.
7. **Edits.** A human edit creates a `human_assertion` claim **born approved** (individually graded), atomically superseding the original — self-review of one's own correction is ceremony. Authority placement per B1 (below registries, above documents): registries contradicting a typed correction surface as contradictions, not silent overrides.
8. **Rejection reasons without garbage labels.** One-tap reject defaults to `low_confidence` (honest, cheap); richer codes one tap deeper; learning weights by specificity × grade; a targeted follow-up ("wrong, or just unproven?") only for high-impact rejections — friction spent exactly where the label is worth most.
9. **Sessions, KPI, rendering.** Per-item resolution persists independently (partial review is a normal state; contradictions stay atomic pairs). KPI: total interaction time per candidate, first open → last member resolved, bucketed by source mix; regressions are bugs. The budget is won in rendering: revisions as **diffs**, contradictions side-by-side with evidence, duplicate-stints as a two-option choice — the UI designs for the specific item types F3 produces, or dies by a thousand generic claim cards.

**Interfaces:** consumes committed batches (F5), items from F3/F7/F9; produces approved beliefs, graded labels (F10), audit evidence (compliance).

## Feature 5 — Multi-Agent Intelligence

**Purpose:** run specialist agents over an extraction artifact + the existing claim graph to produce a complete, evidenced, provenance-correct set of proposed claims — durably, within budget, with every dependent guarantee actually holding.
**Principle (P5):** agents are untrusted components in a harness. Prompts get quality; the harness gets guarantees — every property the system depends on is enforced or measured mechanically, never merely requested.

**Mechanics**
1. **Boundary: agent = one unit of work that does not open a DB session (05 RA-08).** Target architecture: one agent = one Temporal activity (01 §C3). Slice 0: a sync `process_document` command that writes a workspace DTO and returns staged claims is enough; Temporal arrives when a real retry/timeout exists. LangGraph only inside an agent; inter-agent state via persisted workspace records; idempotency via run-scoped output keys (a retried agent overwrites its own staged output — LLM non-determinism must not double-emit).
2. **Run workspace, atomic commit.** Claims stage invisibly; commit at run completion (post-critique) is atomic and is the moment the review batch forms; failed/abandoned runs are GC'd having never existed for scores or review. No half-finished runs polluting provisional scores.
3. **Document-blindness + tiered span validation (E-i11, 05 RA-01).** Only extractors see raw content; downstream agents receive typed claims filtered by relevance — a cross-linker with the CV in context can smuggle fresh "facts" past the evidence gate. At commit, every cited locator must resolve inside its artifact. **Typed tier** (dates, money, closed vocabularies; org aliases only on pass 2 after company resolution): fail blocks commit and increments the hallucination-rate numerator. **Paraphrase tier**: separately measured; typed-fail + paraphrase-pass stages as `proposed` with `paraphrase_only_support`, barred from auto-approval, and does **not** increment the hallucination-rate numerator. Text-layer emails/phones/URLs that are truncated or inconsistent with name tokens raise `possible_ocr_identifier` and are not deterministic identity keys until a human ContactClaim confirms them.
4. **Rubric confidence + calibration gating (B4).** Auto-approval keyed to raw self-reported confidence is a gate keyed to noise that silently re-tunes on every model update. Harness computes confidence from the rubric; per-model calibration is measured on the golden set; a model swap suspends auto-approval for its claims until recalibration passes.
5. **Phase-1 supervisor is code.** Document type → agent DAG is a lookup; the one reserved LLM judgment is "evidence too thin — stop and request more material" (a standing rule: below the floor, output a Decision, never a low-confidence guess). The LLM supervisor arrives with dynamic spawning in Phase 2, against an established eval baseline.
6. **Harness-recorded provenance.** Output schemas *require* cited claim IDs per inferred claim; citations are validated against the actually-served set (citing unserved = rejected output); served-but-uncited claims of consumed types become weak edges so the cascade errs toward re-review. Provenance is a property of the pipe.
7. **Critic: no deletion, different eyes.** The critic downgrades, annotates, or marks `suppressed_by_critic` (retained, hidden by default, auditable) — never deletes; suppressions are sampled in eval (vetoed-claim precision is measured, so an over-suppressing critic gets caught). It runs on a different model family (or at minimum a deliberately different persona) — correlated blind spots are the failure mode of self-review.
8. **Budgets with degradation order (E-i7).** Checked before each call; on exhaustion: drop narrative first, deep research next — never extraction or critique; complete as `budget_truncated`, folded into completeness and mentionable in the Brief. Truncated-but-honest beats failed and unbounded.
9. **Version manifest.** Pinned at run start (prompts, models, ontology, rubric), read by all activities, stamped on every claim — reproducibility, eval attribution, and "which change caused this" become queries.
10. **Partial failure aggregation.** Company branches are child workflows; the parent proceeds with what returned and hands the Critic the explicit missing-branch list — completeness distinguishes *not researched* (retry) from *research found nothing* (`unresolvable`). Transient failures back off; permanent ones (insufficient identity) become Decisions. The contract: best complete picture under the circumstances, gaps named — never all-or-nothing.

**Interfaces:** consumes artifacts (F1), canonical IDs (F2), snapshots (F6); produces committed claim batches (F3/F4); hosts the research extractor (F6) and fit-judgment modules (F7).

## Feature 6 — The Research Gateway

**Purpose:** plan and execute queries across providers and registries for a research goal, returning results as durable, provenance-complete, origin-classified evidence the intelligence layer can safely convert to claims — within budgets and terms, without ingesting someone else's data.
**Principle (P6):** the web is ephemeral (pages change and die), adversarial (content can be planted; fetched pages can carry instructions aimed at models), and unversioned (same query, different truth on different days). Each property gets a countermeasure.

**Mechanics**
1. **Snapshot-or-it-didn't-happen.** Every fetched result becomes a content-addressed **document** (`research_snapshot`, `as_of` = fetch time) with its own extraction artifact; evidence locators point into the snapshot, never the live URL (kept as metadata). Full snapshot bytes are a **TTL cache** (05 RA-04); the durable residue is `evidence.snippet` + content hash + a locator that may go dead. After TTL, re-validation is hash-if-present else snippet-only / unverifiable. This is what makes span validation hold *while the cache is live* — and it deletes the bespoke "evidence pack" object: one pipeline, one guarantee set, with `research_context` (query, provider, goal) as the only addition. Slice 0 does not ingest snapshots.
2. **Gateway never writes claims.** A dedicated **research-extractor agent** — inside the F5 harness with span validation, rubric confidence, workspace staging — treats snapshots exactly like uploads. The company-researcher agent is a *planner* (what to ask; which snapshots matter). The boundary is exact: gateway → documents; harnessed extraction → claims; nothing else.
3. **Provider hygiene, proportionate.** One-time checklist: reputable providers whose standard terms include a DPA (all serious API vendors offer this). One prompt guideline with real substance: never construct queries combining a name with sensitive attributes — the extractor's special-category hygiene applied at the other end of the pipe. No runtime disclosure machinery: the activity log and cost ledger already record which queries ran for which subject as a free byproduct. (Operator-set proportionality standard applies: querying a name on the public web is what every recruiter does daily; the regulation bites on *stored results and profiles*, which the claims/erasure architecture already handles.)
4. **Company anchors, blast-radius-scaled.** Persons have F2's identity precondition; companies get **anchors** (registry number, domain, HQ, industry — one strong or two weak) embedded in queries, and a **relevance validator** discards non-matching snapshots *before* extraction. A contaminated company profile poisons every alumnus — shared objects clear a higher bar.
5. **Adversarial input handling.** Snapshot text is strictly framed as data; instruction-shaped content is flagged, logged as an **injection event**, and quarantined. Domain-reputation weighting; and consequential claims (funding, stage, size) from low-authority web sources **cannot reach approval on a single origin** — second independent origin, or individual review flagged `single_source_web`.
6. **Origin ≠ channel.** The research extractor classifies each snapshot's origin (own profile / employer site / registry / independent press / unknown) as a required, harness-checked output — the candidate's LinkedIn arrives via web search but is candidate-origin: excellent anchor, fine structure, **not a second witness**. Corroboration math consumes origin (B2), or the hole F3 closed reopens through the web.
7. **Honest caching, including absence.** Reused snapshots keep fetch-time `as_of` — stale facts must not masquerade as fresh and win recency contests. **Negative results are cached too** (shorter TTL — absence is volatile): without it, every thin entity re-triggers the same empty searches each enrichment round. Both are Critic-citable ("searched X on date Y, found nothing"), which is what makes `unresolvable_by_research` an evidenced state.
8. **Freshness vs contradiction.** Registry `volatility` decides: volatile disagreement (team size 15 → 120) defaults to temporal bounding — a trajectory, and cross-linker material ("grew 15→120 during the stint"); stable disagreement (founding year) routes to the contradiction path.
9. **Global throttle.** Per-provider token bucket + priority queue (01 §C3); 429s park, never retry-storm; outages degrade to fallback chains or a named gap per F5.10.

**Interfaces:** consumes goals (Critic gaps, verification priorities, manual requests, sourcing); produces snapshot documents into F1's pipeline; extraction happens in F5.

## Feature 7 — Scoring

**Purpose:** convert the claim graph into General Strength and Job Fit outputs that are explainable, comparable where comparison is claimed, reproducible, cheap to keep current — and honest about what they don't know.
**Principle (P7):** a score is a measurement, not a property: instrument (engine version), timestamp (`computed_as_of`), error bar (confidence + coverage), comparability conditions. Forgetting any ingredient is a bug.

**Mechanics**
1. **Time is an input.** Tenure, recency, gap-since features depend on today; a score cached only on `claim_set_hash` rots silently. Fix: `computed_as_of` on every score; time features quantized to month; cache key = hash + as_of_month + engine version; staleness policy (claim change *or* age, whichever first — swept weekly).
2. **Archetype is a reviewable claim.** `TrajectoryArchetypeClaim` (inferred, cited, supersedable) — the highest-leverage judgment in General Strength must not be made by an anonymous classifier; flips arrive as revision proposals, never silent regime changes. Mixed careers get a secondary label; the engine scores the dominant archetype and notes the blend.
3. **Comparability, honestly.** Cross-archetype General Strength comparison is only approximate (different dimension sets) — and doesn't need to be more: **Job Fit is the yardstick** (anchored to requirements, identical for every candidate). GS across archetypes is labeled indicative; the UI never ranks a mixed list by GS alone. Rank-first presentation (below) makes this free.
4. **Coverage floor.** Renormalization (excluding non-assessable dimensions) protects thin documents from zero-fill punishment — but two strong dimensions must not print the same "82" as seven. Below the floor (default 4 of 7 assessable): no composite displayed; breakdown + "insufficient data" (itself information, and a Brief driver). Above: coverage shown beside the number, always. Thinness must neither *penalize* (bias rule) nor *inflate* — the floor threads exactly that needle.
5. **Sanitized embeddings.** Coarse fit's vector is generated from a versioned projection — skills, domains, problem types, seniority signals; **no names, no prestige-carrying institution identities beyond field-of-study, no age-derivable dates** — or every protected signal excluded from features re-enters through similarity. A standing **leakage probe**: embeddings must not predict held-out demographic labels above chance. Projection + probe join the bias documentation.
6. **Per-job adaptive admission.** Global similarity thresholds are meaningless across jobs (a niche role's best match at 0.71; a common role's tenth mediocre match at 0.83): admission = top-K by rank + a floor, per job, tuned on override data. `matched` is relative to *this job's* distribution.
7. **Exclusion needs approval (E-i4).** Coarse fit may read proposed claims for admission/ranking (internal plumbing, labeled provisional); **hard exclusion requires an approved claim**. A proposed deal-breaker (possibly an extraction error) sets `possible_deal_breaker` — visible in breakdown and Brief — without hiding the candidate. Wrong inclusion costs a glance; wrong exclusion costs the placement, invisibly.
8. **No LLM numbers (E-i10).** Fit judgment modules run in the F5 harness and output only categorical per-requirement verdicts (`strong|partial|gap|deal_breaker|UNKNOWN|exceeds_expectations`) with citations; code composes numbers under versioned weights. Every point traces to a deterministic feature or a cited categorical judgment — this one sentence makes "explainable scoring" enforceable. UNKNOWN lowers confidence and generates a question, never lowers the score; its weight is excluded from the per-requirement denominator (04 AL-19). `exceeds_expectations` is distinct from `strong` and routes to the Brief (04 AL-20, 05 RA-10). `score.breakdown` is reserved as the AL-18 shape; Slice 0 does not implement per-requirement full-fit merely to fill it.
9. **Recompute discipline.** Per-candidate recompute is a debounced Temporal singleton (a 40-claim batch approval = one recompute). Shared company-claim changes mark dependent scores **stale** (cheap flag) for lazy recompute prioritized by pipeline activity — an interviewing candidate refreshes in minutes, a dormant one waits for the sweep; no thousand-candidate fan-out because a headcount was corrected.
10. **Environment Fit; geographic plausibility.** Per 01 §F: environment fit is mostly deterministic from claims (stage/type/size/growth history vs the job's environment) with at most one light categorical judgment; personality/values claims are permanently excluded. Geographic plausibility is a Critic check (moderate flag / corridor severity with pre-submission verification gate; company-footprint evidence de-flags), feeding the Brief — history-side geography is plausibility, job-side is the hard filter.
11. **Explanations pin claims** and render through supersession resolution, so a three-week-old explanation still displays coherently; shadow mode (old + new engines side-by-side on golden + live) gates any weight/prompt promotion.

**Interfaces:** consumes approved/proposed claims per tier, archetype claims, sanitized embeddings; produces scores, matched admissions (F8), UNKNOWN/flag items (F9); its bias suite gates F10's learning proposals.

## Feature 8 — Matching & the State Machine

**Purpose:** create and maintain the candidate–job pairs worth attention, and move each through a lifecycle where it is always unambiguous what state holds, whose move it is, what happens on silence, and what was learned at the end.
**Principle (P8):** the pipeline is a multi-party coordination protocol (operator, candidate, client, machine) **with memory** — every state knows who created it, who's awaited, who may unwind it, and what its ending teaches.

**Mechanics**
1. **Pairs are permanent, unique, race-proof.** `UNIQUE(candidate_id, job_id)`, created once, never deleted; both matching workflows (`MatchCandidateToOpenJobs` at `profiled`; `MatchJobToTalentPool` at job open/revision — the moment the accumulated knowledge base pays out) converge via idempotent upsert-events. Permanence is what makes memory (below) possible. On SameAs, pairs **redirect** (`candidate_job.merged_into_id`) rather than rewrite; two pairs on the same job with conflicting human-touched state open a Decision instead of violating the unique constraint (05 RA-03). Company redirects apply the same discipline to `job.hiring_company_id`.
2. **`matched` is a ranked, decaying window.** Un-actioned after N days, or displaced from top-K by stronger arrivals → auto-expire to the `considered` archive — silently, which is legitimate because of the general rule (E-i8): **the machine unwinds only machine-created state; anything human-touched requires a human.**
3. **Requirements revision mid-flight.** Machine-created `matched` failing the revised coarse fit auto-expires; human-touched pairs are re-scored and flagged (*"no longer meets: [which]"*) as Decisions — the client may waive for someone they've met; auto-withdrawal would be the machine unwinding human work, and possibly wrong.
4. **Structured outcomes.** Every terminal transition records `{party, reason}` — client-rejected / candidate-withdrew / operator-passed(cause) / job_filled / job_cancelled / lost_to_other_offer / placed. Four opposite meanings must not collapse into "rejected": these outcomes are the ground-truth labels learning requires, collected for the cost of one enum at a moment the human is already acting.
5. **Job lifecycle with cascade.** `open → on_hold → filled | cancelled`. On `filled`: in-flight pairs end `job_filled` (nothing was wrong with them); communication Decisions for late-stage candidates; **silver medalists** (late-stage reached) are immediately re-matched against similar open jobs and enter top-priority nurture — the near-chosen candidate is the best-qualified lead the knowledge base holds; letting them evaporate discards the pipeline's highest-value output.
6. **Cross-pair events.** `placed` on A → Decisions on every other active pair (not auto-withdraw — placements fall through). `offer` anywhere → **competing-offer flags on all active pairs**: the strongest urgency lever in recruiting, known to the system before the operator — surfaced, never buried in per-pair isolation.
7. **Timers belong to the awaited party.** Each state declares `awaiting: operator|candidate|client|nobody`. Awaiting-candidate timeout → nudge (later a PA draft); awaiting-client → chase Decision; **awaiting-operator → the operator's own accountability surfaces** ("submission ready, unsent 3 days") — the system holds its user to the same standard, which is half the value of timers. Party activity resets their timer; timers pause with resume dates; a timer firing for an exited state validates and no-ops — timers advise, the state machine decides.
8. **Optimistic concurrency.** Every transition event carries expected current state; mismatch → rejected with refresh. Boring, mandatory, previously absent.
9. **Memory.** Terminal pairs carry outcome + causally-linked claim IDs; **re-proposal only when those claims change**, delivered with the explanation ("previously passed for no-GCP; new evidence: …"). `we_passed` is soft memory. **Client rejection is client-scoped** — re-submitting a client-rejected candidate to the *same client's* next job is a hard block at (candidate, hiring company) with explicit-override only: the error class that ends agency relationships. (Client-as-account distinct from hiring company: named ADR, deferred — memory keys on `hiring_company_id` for now.)
10. **Nurture is behavior, not a label.** Entry: silver medalist; strong-general/no-current-fit. Standing: elevated re-match priority; periodic claim-staleness refresh; later, PA touch sequences. Exit: matched onward, or stale → archived. Undefined, nurture is where candidates go to be forgotten; defined, it is where the knowledge base compounds into future placements.

**Interfaces:** consumes admissions (F7), events (operator, PA, inbound); produces outcome labels (F10), urgency flags (F9), nurture cohort (F10 sequences).

## Feature 9 — Screening Brief & Compliance

**Purpose (Brief):** compile everything the system doesn't know, doesn't trust, or can't verify into a call agenda — and capture the answers back as the highest-authority evidence the system holds.
**Principle (P9):** the Brief is the interface of a loop, not a report. The operator's call is where human assertions are born; treating the Brief as one-way output breaks the flywheel.

**Mechanics — Brief**
1. **Compiler, not agent.** Deterministic assembly from existing flags and statuses (Critic gaps, UNKNOWN requirements, contradictions, plausibility/geographic/corridor flags, extraordinary-unverified, competing-offer, availability/comp/authorization, pair-outcome memory). A "brief agent" re-reasoning over the graph would be a second intelligence that can disagree with the first — the worst property for a trust product. Every item traces to the flag that produced it; the only LLM involvement is phrasing.
2. **Items have identity and lifecycle.** Keyed to their source flag: `open → asked → answered | dismissed | expired`. Regeneration is **reconciliation** — new flags add, resolved flags retire, surviving items keep state; an item auto-resolves when its underlying flag resolves. Without this, regeneration-from-scratch resurrects answered questions, the operator stops trusting the Brief within two calls, and the flywheel dies. (This corrects a genuine bug in the prior blueprint's "regenerate on claim-set change.")
3. **Two scopes, inherited not copied.** Candidate-scope items (identity, plausibility, comp) vs pair-scope (job-specific UNKNOWNs); pair Briefs *inherit* open candidate items — answered anywhere, resolved everywhere. A candidate on three jobs is asked their notice period once.
4. **`internal_reason` vs `question`.** The corridor check's blunt rationale must never render candidate-facing; every item carries both fields, phrasing template-anchored per flag type, ordering deliberately simple (severity within three conversational sections: opening — motivation/availability; middle — probes/unknowns; close — logistics/comp). Anything fancier over-engineers a conversation the operator will run their own way.
5. **Inline answer capture — the return path.** Answering an item on the Brief (*"reports to CTO, UK hours — confirmed"*) creates a born-approved `human_assertion` in one action, resolving the item and refreshing scores immediately. Uploaded call notes remain the rich path; inline capture is the frictionless one that makes the flywheel spin at conversation speed — the system's best evidence accumulating exactly where its uncertainty was highest. Phase-1 requirement, not polish.

**Mechanics — Compliance (proportionate by operator standard)**
6. **Art. 14 collapses into existing machinery.** Notification for sourced candidates is satisfied by the first outreach containing the privacy-notice link — which the outreach gate already enforces. Remaining machinery: the daily sweep finds sourced-never-contacted candidates approaching one month → one Decision: **contact now (notice included) or delete.** One footer, one query, zero new systems.
7. **Erasure: one workflow, one checklist, one verification.** Scope fully enumerated (E-i9, 05 RA-04); implemented as a single workflow ending in a verification query that fails loudly on any survivor — the whole difference between "we have an erasure feature" and "erasure works." Single-subject documents delete. Multi-subject live snapshots follow RA-04 (TTL-force or named conflict — the verify query must not report green). Subject-access export falls out free: the claim/evidence structure *is* the export, generated on demand. Slice 0 tests single-subject only.
8. **Retention: one rule.** E.g. 24 months since last meaningful activity; one sweep; one Decision type (erase or refresh — renewed contact restarts the clock). No per-claim schedules, no tiered matrices: a solo operator with working erasure and a working sweep exceeds most funded competitors; building further is procrastination in a legal costume.

**Interfaces:** consumes every flag producer; produces questions to the operator and, via capture + inbound replies (F10), the strongest label class F4/F10 receive.

## Feature 10 — PA Mode & Preference Learning

**Purpose:** progressively take over coordination (messages, follow-ups, scheduling) at exactly the pace demonstrated trust allows, while converting every correction, response, and outcome into signal that makes next week's system better.
**Principle (P10):** outreach is conversations, not actions — two-way, interruptible, and itself evidence. Silence is the only state in which automation may proceed. On the learning side: the system learns *the operator*, not the truth, from labels that are byproducts of attention.

**Mechanics — PA**
1. **Outward projection (E-i12).** Drafts generate only from: approved claims about the recipient, public job facts, Brief `question` fields, conversation history — structurally served, so scores, flags, `internal_reason`, and third parties are absent by construction, not by prompt-discouragement. One leaked "our system flagged your employment claims" ends the relationship.
2. **Inbound rules (E-i12).** Any inbound message halts all automation on its thread instantly, at every rung. Inbound is classified (`interested|question|not_now|stop|bounce`): `stop` **writes the suppression registry** (its previously missing producer); `interested` raises an urgent Decision; resumption of any sequence after a reply requires a human touch. Outbound automation without inbound handling is a machine for burning relationships — the day-9 follow-up after "please stop" (or after "yes, call me!") is the fastest way to destroy both the candidate relationship and the operator's trust.
3. **Replies are documents.** Inbound routes through standard ingestion (`email_thread`; the splitter handles threads), emerges as fresh candidate-authored claims, resolves Brief items, refreshes scores. Conversation-as-evidence costs zero new machinery — just a route.
4. **Autonomy ladder with honest promotion.** Rungs per action *type*: 0 suggest → 1 draft (human sends) → 2 auto after per-item approval → 3 auto, digest-reported, undoable → 4 sequences. Launch at 0–1. Promotion counts **unedited/lightly-edited sends** (edit-distance threshold), ≥95% over ≥50 instances + explicit opt-in — raw "acceptance" of drafts the operator rewrote measures clicking, not trust. The draft-vs-sent **diff** is the richest writing-style label available, captured free.
5. **Real undo.** Rung-3 irrevocable actions (sends) release through a **pending queue** with a visible delay window (30–60 min, pending tray; digest reports sent vs pending separately); undo = pull from queue. Reversible actions (calendar holds, state moves) skip it. "Instant undo" for an email already sent is a lie; the queue makes it true.
6. **Sequences are state behavior, not an engine.** Nurture/follow-up sequences = per-state PA behavior config on F8's party-aware timers, halted by state transitions (candidate reaches `screening` → sequence dead) and by rule 2. A standalone scheduler would duplicate the state machine and then fight it. The three send gates (Art. 14 notice, representation/contact-permission, suppression) are hard preconditions in the send path.
7. **Daily digest = compiler** (open Decisions, fired timers, pending/sent trays, competing-offer flags, sweep results). The Brief pattern, reapplied; nothing to design.

**Mechanics — Learning**
8. **Personalization, named honestly.** Outcomes exist only for pursued candidates; a model trained on them learns what the operator already likes and cannot discover they're wrong about a category. Frame the loop as personalization (the product promise anyway) and keep the counterfactual visible: the `considered` archive records matched-never-pursued — exactly the population any future "you may be systematically passing on X" analysis needs, logged free today.
9. **Bias gate on learned changes.** Weekly proposals (weight tweaks, prompt refinements, anti-requirement templates) pass F7's bias/leakage suite before going live — otherwise correlated overrides get encoded wearing the legitimacy of "learned from data." Prompt refinements — free text that can smuggle proxies past numeric checks — additionally get the golden-set demographic-slice comparison.
10. **Label hygiene.** Recency decay (preferences drift); minimum-cluster floor (three rejections are an anecdote, not a pattern); **proposals cite their motivating labels** — reviewed exactly like a claim citing evidence, the same pattern once more. Every input is graded (F4), specificity-weighted, snapshot-contextualized (claims shown, scores, versions — captured synchronously at decision time, because reconstructed context is the wrong context).

**Interfaces:** consumes gates and cohorts (F8), questions (F9), graded labels (F4), outcomes (F8); produces suppression entries, style labels, config proposals gated by F7's suite and F4's review.

## Feature 11 — Business Development

**Purpose:** discover active jobs at companies the operator has no relationship with, and convert the existing candidate base into new client relationships through a two-stage, two-sided-anonymous funnel — while simultaneously using every outreach as an active refresh instrument for the knowledge base.
**Principle (P11):** the recruiter's economic position *is* an information-flow rule — hold the introduction on both sides until both sides have committed interest. **Anonymized in writing, revealed only in conversation:** company name to the candidate on the call; candidate identity to the company only at submission with consent. Written artifacts carry generalized attributes; names move exclusively through live, relationship-building channels.

**Why this feature is nearly free:** it is not a new system but a new loop through built machinery — job discovery is a research-tier extension, matching is origin-agnostic, outreach inherits the entire PA apparatus (ladder, projection, gates, inbound-halt, pending queue), replies are documents, and the Brief drives the calls. Roughly 80% exists; the 20% below is what's new.

**Mechanics**
1. **Job discovery as a research-tier extension.** Company research additionally collects active postings. Most startup career pages run on a handful of ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable) exposing **structured public job-board endpoints** — for a large share of targets this is clean JSON, not scraping; detect the ATS during research, pull postings, refresh on the volatile-data cadence. Each posting flows into `JobIntelligence` exactly like a briefed JD (requirement claims, job-level resolution handling reposts/revisions, pool re-matching) with `origin = discovered` — **discovered jobs never silently enter the client pipeline**; they are leads.
2. **Matching costs nothing more; the trigger is tunable.** `MatchJobToTalentPool` is origin-agnostic and coarse fit is a database query (E-i14), so discovered jobs multiply the pair space at marginal compute — the literal mechanics of "10× utilization." A strong pair against a non-client company is the BD trigger, with two distinct conditions producing two distinct email genres: one exceptional MPC above a high bar ("I have exactly your person") vs. N solid candidates above a lower bar ("I have a bench").
3. **The two-stage funnel — candidate-first is stage one, not an alternative.** Teaser to the candidate (company anonymized: domain, stage, location) → interested reply → refresher call (name revealed *on the call*; Brief-driven; inline capture) → confirmed interest → **now** the company-side spec email fires, warm: "a senior candidate, currently in your space, who has expressed interest in exactly this role" is a different genre from a cold bench pitch. The cold email stops being cold — stage two converts because stage one armed it.
4. **Anonymization is a harness problem (P5), not a prompt instruction — and pool-size is the wrong test (05 RA-05).**
   *Candidate spec* (to companies): generated from approved claims through an extended outward projection, then generalized by **conservative fixed rules** (institution → "a top UK university"; the 15→120 growth story → "joined pre-Series-A, stayed through scale"). Searching our own talent pool for “how many people match this spec” is **not** the safety mechanism — our pool is not the adversary’s universe. Human review is a standing gate before any spec sends. Rich cross-linked profiles are near-fingerprints.
   *Company teaser* (to candidates): generalized on domain/geography by the same class of fixed rules, calibrated later on small-market cases (Berlin seed climate-tech, not London fintech). Cardinality against the discovered-jobs corpus is a tuning aid, not a ship gate.
   *Recipient exclusion (mechanical, ships with Feature 11):* the candidate's **current employer and immediate ecosystem never receive their spec** — a hiring manager recognizing their own employee in an anonymized spec is career-endingly bad and trivially preventable, since employment claims say exactly whom to exclude.
5. **The eligibility sweep — a triple-product scheduler.** Candidate-first sends are selected weekly by **staleness of current-status claims** (not registration age — someone registered a year ago but spoken to last month is fresh; the trigger is unverified availability/status older than ~4–6 months, tuned on reply data) **× match strength × prospect-client value**, gated by: `marketable` permission, no active-pipeline conflict (a candidate at `interviewing` on a briefed job receives no teaser — the system must not compete with itself; F8's cross-pair machinery enforces this), and the standard send gates. This one scheduler simultaneously refreshes the most-decayed valuable profiles, tests the most winnable BD opportunities, and spends scarce outreach capacity (deliverability caps are real) where compound return is highest.
6. **Warmth gates the template.** By design the teaser lands on a warm-kept candidate, for whom "I've got something interesting — 15 minutes this week?" is a normal message from a trusted person; confidentiality ("one of my clients, a Series-A fintech here in Berlin") is the professional norm, and naming the company in writing would signal amateurism while handing the candidate a route around. For high-value **cold-stale** profiles (no recent genuine touch), the correct sequence is a two-step: plain reconnection first, opportunity teaser second. Nurture and BD are one system — nurture makes the teaser cheap; the teaser makes nurture worth doing.
7. **The refresh instrument — every reply branch yields claims.** Interested → refresher call → the richest single update a dormant profile can receive (Brief items answered, availability confirmed, motivation and comp updated) plus a live MPC for stage two. "Not looking, I'm at NewCo" → a fresh CareerStepClaim from the highest-authority source (the person, about themselves, today): old stint bounded, NewCo researched, everything downstream refreshed. "Not this, but keep me in mind for X" → MotivationClaim update. Bounce → the contact claim falsified. Silence → a decay signal feeding channel selection. The system pays for knowledge-base freshness *with opportunities instead of check-in spam* — and because the ask is a call, the intelligence yield per success is multi-item (inline capture), not single-fact. Replies are documents (F10.3); the machinery exists, this gives it mail.
8. **`marketable` is a Brief item.** Collected on the first call ("open to me representing you proactively beyond this role?") — one question, near-universal yes, and it prevents the credibility burn of pitching an MPC who then declines. No spec fires on a stale availability claim: the pitch is only as good as the bench is current.
9. **Segments — named, materialized bench strength.** A segment ("GTM · Senior · seed–Series-A · Berlin") is a saved query over claims (function × seniority × stage-experience × location), membership refreshed by incremental sweeps. Three uses in ascending strategic value: BD targeting (a discovered job matches a segment — the segment *is* the pitched bench); receipts-backed sales narrative ("12 senior applied-AI engineers, fintech-startup experience, NY — 8 with verified availability under 30 days" is a claim backed by claims); and the **supply×demand map** — segments × counts × strength × freshness is the supply side of the operator's own platform, discovered jobs are the live demand side of the market, and their intersection tells the operator where to source, which verticals to specialize in, and which BD emails to send this week. BD quietly becomes the platform's strategy instrument.
10. **Client relationship lifecycle.** BD gives companies states — `prospect → contacted → in_conversation → active_client → dormant` — plus, in time, contacts (hiring-manager discovery is a well-trodden B2B provider category slotting into the research gateway; B2B prospecting needs no compliance machinery beyond the proportionality standard — legitimate-interest business outreach is the most settled ground there is) and do-not-contact status. This is the client-as-account ADR (03 §5) arriving for an offensive reason — winning business — rather than a defensive one; healthier that way.

**Interfaces:** consumes company research (F6), pool matching (F8/E-i14), staleness sweeps + nurture (F8), Brief items + inline capture (F9), the full PA send path and reply ingestion (F10); produces client relationships, refreshed claims, warm specs, segments, and the supply×demand view. Its two validators extend F10's outward projection; its funnel outcomes join the learning label stream.
